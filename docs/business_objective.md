\# Business Objective



\## Business Problem



Maruti Suzuki needs a data driven approach to monitor sales performance across products, dealers, geographic regions and sales channels. The organisation also needs to understand how pricing and discount strategies along with the booking to delivery time influence the customer satisfaction and sales outcomes. The analysis will also help to identify the potential regions and sales channel where discounts, promotional activities or marketing campaigns can be employed to improve the sales. In addition to variation in booking to delivery time will be investigated to identify if any potential operational gaps are present in the given region or sales channel needs to be addressed. Addressing these following areas can significantly improve the sales performance, customer satisfaction, dealer effectiveness, regional growth and overall operational efficiency.



\## Project Objective



The objective of the project is to:

&#x20;

1. Analyze five-year sales trends across products, models, regions, and sales channels, and evaluate the performance and market share of different product specifications such as fuel type, transmission, and colour.
2. Assess dealer and sales channel performance across regions and identify underperforming and high-potential regions, products, and models to identify opportunities for sales improvement.
3. Evaluate the relationship between discounts, booking-to-delivery time, and customer satisfaction, and assess whether differences in financing partners are associated with variations in customer satisfaction.
4. Evaluate the contribution of individual, corporate, and fleet customers to overall sales, identify high-potential customer segments, and provide data-driven recommendations to support targeted marketing and promotional strategies.
